Natural Earth publishes its raster and vector map data in the public domain.
The data may be modified and distributed for personal, educational and
commercial purposes. Crediting the authors is not required.

This deployment includes derived country boundaries (1:50m Admin 0 Countries,
v5.1.1) and city points/names (1:10m Populated Places, v5.1.2), downloaded
2026-10-18. Primary authors: Tom Patterson, Nathaniel Vaughn Kelso and the
Natural Earth contributors. Source metadata and transformation instructions
are maintained in frontend/src/assets/location-map/README.md.
